How it heats

    Manual tune

    1. °Your temperatureWhat this button heats the room to
    2. 1Heating upPower level while the room is cold
    3. 2Getting closeStart easing down this many degrees below your temperature
    4. 3At temperatureLevel it holds at once the room is warm (it stays on)
